## The Wall Street Crash:

- The American stock market crashes
  --> This causes American banks to recall their loans to Germany
  --> This leaves many Germans unemployed and the government bankrupt
- Most importantly, the government did not take any decisive action

This indecision left the public angry with the incoherent nature of the proportional representation system that German elections employ.

This gave the Nazis a perfect situation to exploit for their needs, arguably without which, they wouldn't have come to power.

## Hitler becomes Chancellor:

- Heinrich Bruning becomes Chancellor. He attempts to raise taxes and cut benefits to payoff the Americans.
  --> He uses Article 48 100 times between 1930-1932
- In July 1932, Hindenburg dismisses Bruning and appoints von Papen to be chancellor.
  --> He also needs to use Article 48
- In November 1932, Hindenburg is tired of using undemocratic emergency powers, and calls another election, hoping von Papen will gain a majority.
  --> Instead, the Nazis win and von Papen stays in power
- In December, Hindenburg dismisses von Papen. He has to choose between Hitler, Thalman or Shleiker.
  --> von Shleiker is appointed as chancellor
- In January 1933, von Papen suggests to Hindenburg that he can rein in Hitler and keep him in the pocket of the CDU
  --> Hindenburg then decides to appoint Hitler
