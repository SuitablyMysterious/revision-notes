
*Hydrocarbon* - molecule comprising solely hydrogen and carbon
*Homolagous series* - group of organic compounds that have a common structure and similar properties
*Saturated* - contains only $\ce{C\bond{1}C}$ bonds
*Unsaturated* - contains $\ce{C\bond{2}C}$  or $\ce{C\bond{3}C}$ bonds
*Polar* - has a difference in charge across the molecule (eg. $\ce{H\delta+\bond{1}O^{\delta-}\bond{1}H\delta+}$)

## Alkanes:

 - a homolagous series of hydrocarbons
 - has the general formula $\ce{C_nH_{2n+2}}$
 - they are saturated

#### Properties:

As the length of the carbon chain increases:
- boiling point increases
- viscosity increases
- flammability decreases

#### Combustion Reactions:

###### Complete:

$$\ce{C3H8 + 5O2 -> 3CO2 + 4H2O}$$
$$\ce{CH3\bond{1}CH2\bond{1}CH3 + 5O\bond{2}O -> 3O\bond{2}C\bond{2}O + H\bond{1}O\bond{1}H}$$
###### Incomplete:
$$\ce{C3H8 + 2O2 -> C + 2CO + 4H2O}$$
$$\ce{CH3\bond{1}CH2\bond{1}CH3 + 2O\bond{2}O -> C + 2:O\bond{2}C: + H\bond{1}O\bond{1}H}$$
#### Tests:

To test for $\ce{CO2}$, bubble through lime water (it goes cloudy if present)
To test for $\ce{H2O}$, drop onto blue cobalt chloride paper (it goes pink if present)

#### Environmental Impact:

$\ce{CO2}$ is a greenhouse gas, meaning that it contributes to the entrapment of heat in the Earth's atmosphere; producing global warming.
$\ce{CO}$ displaces $\ce{O2}$ in red blood cells, causing suffocation.
$\ce{C}$ is a particulate, and can cause harm to the lungs as smoke inhalation

#### Cracking:

- splits large chain hydrocarbons into smaller, more useful fractions and compounds called Alkenes
- a thermal decomposition reaction

It is done industrially through steam or catalytic cracking.

## Alkenes:

 - a homolagous series of hydrocarbons
 - has the general formula $\ce{C_nH_{2n}}$
 - they are unsaturated

They are much more reactive as they have polar double bonds between carbons and a higher electron density.
